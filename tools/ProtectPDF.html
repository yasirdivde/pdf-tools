Password Protect PDF

Encrypt your PDF with AES-256. Anyone opening the file will need the password you set. Everything happens in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

  • AES-256 encryption — the industry standard
  • Strong password generator built in
  • 100% browser-based — your files never leave your device

Everything You Need to
Protect a PDF

AES-256 encryption

Your PDF is protected with the same encryption standard used by banks and government agencies. The file will require your password to open — nothing else will.

Built-in strong password generator

Not sure what to use? Tap Generate and we’ll create a cryptographically strong 12-character password. Save it somewhere safe — it can’t be recovered.

Private by design

Your document and password never leave your device. All encryption happens in your browser — we can’t access your file even if we wanted to.

How To Protect a PDF
Online for Free

  1. 1

    Drop the PDF you want to protect into the upload zone.

  2. 2

    Type a password (or click Generate for a strong one) and confirm it.

  3. 3

    Click Encrypt PDF and wait a few seconds.

  4. 4

    Download your password-protected PDF. Save the password — it cannot be recovered.
