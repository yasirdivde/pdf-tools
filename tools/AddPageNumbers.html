Add Page Numbers to PDF

Number every page with full control over position, format, color, and size — right in your browser. Free, secure, and no installation needed.

  • 6 positions · 8 formats · custom color, size & margin
  • Skip the cover page, or start numbering from any value
  • 100% browser-based — your files never leave your device

Everything You Need to
Number a PDF

Six positions, eight formats

Place numbers at any corner or center edge, and choose from plain digits, “Page 1 of 100”, roman numerals, letters, or a custom prefix.

Your color, your size

Pick any color with the native picker and dial in the exact font size and margin you need. Bold toggle included, so numbers fit any layout.

Private by design

Your PDF is processed entirely inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or shared — your document stays completely under your control.
